Scientific Methodology

Two Distinct Clinical Research Tasks

Decoupling hepatic ultrasonic classification from high-resolution CT segmentation ensures modality-specific rigor.

🔬

TASK A: Ultrasound Classification

Classification

Primary focus on differentiating Healthy Parenchyma from Hepatic Steatosis (Fatty Liver) using acoustic attenuation and texture descriptors.

Target Evaluation Metrics: Accuracy, Sensitivity (Recall), Specificity, ROC-AUC, and Confusion Matrix.
🩻

TASK B: CT Liver & Tumor Segmentation

Segmentation

Volumetric identification of parenchymal contours and localized focal neoplasms (HCC) on contrast-enhanced CT scans, validated against the LiTS benchmark.

Target Evaluation Metrics: Dice Similarity Coefficient, Intersection over Union (IoU), and Surface Distance.

Six Neural Network Architectures

Benchmarking state-of-the-art vision backbones against our verified DenseNet-121 baseline.

VGG16

Baseline Stack

138.4M parameters. Deep 3x3 convolution stacks establishing baseline acoustic feature capture.

VGG19

Villanueva et al.

143.7M parameters. Extended 19-layer hierarchy evaluated across 1,895 ultrasound images.

ResNet50V2

Residual

25.6M parameters. Pre-activation identity shortcuts enabling gradient propagation without degradation.

DenseNet-201

Planned

20.0M parameters. 201-layer deep feature reuse maximizing multi-scale representation efficiency.

Inception-ResNet-v2

Planned

55.8M parameters. Dual multiscale filter factorizations merged with residual skip pathways.

Multi-Scale CNN

PMID: 34097226

Specialized dilated and multiscale kernels tailored for diffuse hepatic ultrasound speckle variance.
