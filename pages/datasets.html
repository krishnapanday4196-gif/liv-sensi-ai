Class Distribution & Target Classes

Scientific Rigor
CLASS 1: NORMAL
Healthy Parenchyma

Smooth margins, normal portal vascularity, absence of beam attenuation.

CLASS 2: STEATOSIS
Fatty Liver (NAFLD)

Diffusely increased acoustic echogenicity with deep acoustic beam attenuation.

CLASS 3: CIRRHOSIS
Chronic Fibrosis

Coarse heterogeneous parenchymal texture and irregular nodular surface.

CLASS 4: TUMOR / HCC
Focal Neoplasm

Focal mass with peripheral hypervascularity and heterogeneous internal necrotic core.
