Grounding our platform architecture against verified published works in ultrasound classification and CT segmentation.

Study 1 • Ultrasound NAFLD

Multi-scale CNN for Hepatic Ultrasound

Identifier: PMID: 34097226
LOOCV Validated
Reported Methodology:

Multi-feature guided multi-scale residual CNN architecture designed to capture both micro-speckle parenchymal variations and macro-acoustic beam attenuation.

Reported Literature Finding:
Accuracy > 90% | AUC 97.8%
Published Result
Study 2 • MRI-PDFF Reference

Byra et al. (2021)

Source: Journal of Ultrasound in Medicine
135 Subjects
Reported Methodology:

Transfer-learning convolutional neural network evaluated across four standard ultrasound views from 135 individuals, calibrated against MRI-PDFF (Proton Density Fat Fraction) reference truth.

Reported Literature Finding:
AUC 0.90 for Fatty Liver
Published Result
Study 3 • Deep VGG19 Transfer

Villanueva et al. (SPP 2021)

Dataset: 1,895 B-Mode Ultrasound Images
5 Disease Classes
Reported Methodology:

VGG19 deep convolutional transfer learning architecture optimized for multi-class liver disease categorization across 1,895 clinical ultrasound images.

Reported Literature Finding:
86.7% Test Accuracy
Published Result
